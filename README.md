# First Things First

When people say your 20s is for you to explore, learn, and build something, then why are we suddenly facing rising inflation, accelerating global warming, a skyrocketing housing market, and fewer job vacancies!? Where are we heading? One good thing about Gen-Z is that we are born to be problem-solvers, and I am on a mission to design a tiny, modular, off-grid, & sustainable house to remove any financial burden on me.

# What things do I need in my house?

A bedroom, bathroom, and kitchen are must in a home, as they are the absolute core essentials of a functional home. Some might not agree with me, but I think a tiny house should have at most 2 bedrooms, a bathroom, a combined living room with a dining area, and an open kitchen inside.
